How to

          
        

        

        

          
Start the mirror
Press Start and allow camera and microphone access. Your reflection is mirrored. If microphone access is unavailable, the cloud button still works.

          
Fog it up
Stay quiet for a second to calibrate, then blow steadily toward your microphone. Fog gathers near your mouth. You can also hold the cloud button or Space.

          
Write in the fog
With lipstick off, click and drag with your mouse or touchpad, or drag a finger on the screen. Your strokes clear the fog.

          
Draw with your hand
Hold up your index finger with the other fingers curled, then move it to write. Open your hand to stop drawing. Make a fist and move it to wipe a wider area of fog.

          
Use lipstick
Click the lipstick tube to turn lipstick on. Draw with your index finger, touch screen, or click-and-drag on your touchpad. Click the tube again to return to fog clearing.

          
Choose a shade
While lipstick is on, click a shade in the side palette (at the top in landscape). Or hold your tracked fingertip over a shade until the white ring fills. Existing marks keep their original colors.

          
Adjust your brush
Move the slider to change the drawing size. The hand button switches to a broad fog wipe. You can also hold Shift or Alt while dragging to wipe broadly. Wiping fog does not erase lipstick.

          
Add stickers
Click the sticker button at the bottom, then choose an image. Drag a placed sticker with your mouse, touchpad, or touch screen to move it. Select it and use Size to resize, or the trash button to remove it. Click the checkmark or press Esc to finish. Reopen Stickers to edit again. Arrow keys move a focused sticker; Delete removes it.

          
Clear the glass
Click Clear or press C to remove all fog, lipstick, and stickers. Blowing again adds fresh fog.

          
Take a photo
Click the red circle or press S. A PNG of your reflection, fog, lipstick, and stickers downloads to your device. Buttons, instructions, selection outlines, and drawing cursors are not included.

          
Microphone and readouts
If the microphone button appears, click it to enable or resume audio. Press D to show or hide microphone and tracking readouts. Drawing pauses breath-triggered fog so new fog does not cover your stroke.

        

        
Keyboard: Space = fog · S = photo · C = clear · D = readouts
Esc closes this guide. Outside the guide, Esc returns to the start screen. Drawing pauses while this guide is open.
